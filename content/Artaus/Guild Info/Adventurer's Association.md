### The Adventurers’ Association

Motto: “For Gold, Glory, and the Greater Good.”

#### Overview:

The Adventurers’ Association is a city-spanning guild with local branches in each of Celest’s seven districts. Formed during the Age of Rising Purpose, the Association was originally a coalition of independent wanderers and scholars who sought to formalize their efforts under one banner. Today, it acts as a regulated hub for handling civilian and governmental contracts that require skilled individuals—particularly those attuned by the Spires.

Whether someone needs rare alchemical reagents from the Spire of Enchantment, an escort through the wilderness between districts, or a lost heirloom recovered from the depths of a crypt, the Adventurers’ Association serves as the go-to intermediary between need and deed.

---

#### Primary Functions:

- Contract Management:  
      
    Facilitates quests ranging from minor delivery jobs to dangerous retrievals deep within spire depths. Tasks are ranked by tier and posted to district branches.  
      
    
- Spire Resource Recovery:  
      
    Organizes sanctioned excursions into spires for the collection of rare components (e.g., enchanted flora, relics, volatile crystals).  
      
    
- Investigation and Tracking:  
      
    Accepts jobs requiring the location of lost persons, runaway constructs, or stolen goods within and across districts.  
      
    
- Academic Collaborations:  
      
    Partners with institutions like the Moonsunder Library or Lantern Archive to investigate spire anomalies, divine traces, or celestial relics.  
      
    
- Mentorship and Attunement Support:  
      
    Offers training programs and pairings between junior adventurers and seasoned veterans, often preparing youth for their Rite of Passage or helping them understand their attunement more deeply.  
      
    

---

#### Structure and Ranks:

- Grand Board of Seven:  
      
    One representative from each district, ensuring district interests are preserved. Oversees high-tier quest approvals and guild-wide policy.  
      
    
- Questmaster (Local Branch Leaders):  
      
    Runs day-to-day operations at each branch. Approves contracts, ranks adventurers, and manages resources.  
      
    
- Guild Tiers:  
      
    

- Copper Tier – Entry-level jobs (e.g., deliveries, minor errands).  
      
    
- Silver Tier – Standard fieldwork (e.g., spire fetch quests, bodyguard work).  
      
    
- Gold Tier – High-risk contracts, spire incursions, or political sensitivity.  
      
    
- Platinum Tier – Reserved for elite adventurers. Tasks may involve inter-district affairs or relic-level threats.  
      
    

Membership & dues

  

Membership cost is 25gp per person

  

Members must register their attunement 

Member ranks are based off from individual merit although contracts can be completed as a group

  

Dues- dues are paid based off of membership tier and are taken as a percentage of payment on completed contracts.

Copper- 25%

Silver- 20%

Gold- 10%

Platinum -5%
