### The Climbers’ Society

Motto: “To Climb is to Become.”

#### Overview:

The Climbers’ Society is the most specialized of the three great guilds, singularly dedicated to the art, risk, and glory of spire ascension. Unlike the Rite of Passage—often a one-time journey of self-discovery—members of the Society are career climbers. They seek not only to survive the spires, but to master them. Every floor conquered is a mark of pride. Every record broken is a mark in history. And for a rare few, the peak of a Spire is not the end, but only the beginning.

Founded in the early days after the Spires first rose across Celest, the Society emerged to support those who returned to the towers again and again, unraveling their mysteries and overcoming their trials. Now, it stands as the beating heart of spire culture, adrenaline, and fame.

---

#### Guild Purpose & Services:

- Spire Climb Tracking:  
      
    Uses enchanted Spire Stones attuned to each guild member. These stones track:  
      
    

- Highest floor reached per spire  
      
    
- Climber party size and name  
      
    
- Record of spire-specific feats  
      
    Leaderboards are posted in each district hall and updated weekly.  
      
    

- Renown System:  
      
    Fame within the Society is everything. Titles, ceremonial sashes, and bronze-to-platinum sigils denote one’s climbing reputation. Top climbers are household names, celebrated (or envied) throughout their districts.  
      
    
- Climber Support Services:  
      
    

- Return Tokens – Allow a party to safely exit a spire under duress.  
      
    
- Spire Keys – Grant access to previously cleared floor thresholds for training or speed-climbs.  
      
    
- Echo Tokens – Echoes of previously conquered floors to prepare for harder challenges.  
      
    
- Sanctioned Return Halls – Located near every spire, offering free medical care, restoration magic, and rest services.  
      
    
- Challenge tokens – tokens that can be used upon entering a spire or ascending a floor to grant harder challenges for better rewards
    

---

#### Organization & Locations:

- Local Guildhalls (District-Based):  
      
    Each district has its own branch of the Society located adjacent to their respective spire. These branches are modest in scale but full of character—walls lined with climbing records, commemorative plaques, and magical floor maps.  
      
    
- High Celest – The Apex Archive:  
      
    A hidden vault said to house records of all known peak attempts, along with testimonials from the rare few who’ve climbed farther than any others. Only the Society’s upper echelon—The Echobound Circle—have access to this sanctum.  
      
    

---

#### Guild Structure:

- The Echobound Circle:  
      
    The secretive and elite upper tier of the Society. Composed only of climbers who have reached some of the upper known floors. They rarely reveal themselves, but their decisions shape policy and distribute rare keys and boons.  
      
    
- Floor Marshals:  
      
    Officers of each district guildhall. Oversee challenges, validate climbs, award rank sigils, and maintain the local leaderboard.  
      
    
- Climbers:  
      
    Members of all ranks. From fledgling rookies with nothing but a dream to hardened veterans with ascension records, all belong equally to the Society.  
      
    

---

#### Cultural Role:

To join the Climbers’ Society is to step into a life of challenge, camaraderie, and risk. Some join to chase greatness. Others to train for their Rite. A few seek answers the spires have buried within. But all understand: the climb changes you. Whether or not you reach the top.

### Token System & Pricing

Tokens are not for sale on the open market and must be purchased through the Climbers’ Society with earned Renown Points or at specific guild-approved auctions.

---

#### Token Types:

1. Return Tokens – Teleport party out of a spire.  
      
    

- Cost: 250 gp  
      
    
- Availability: Rare – only available to Adept tier and above.  
      
    

3. Spire Keys – Allow entry at previously cleared floors.  
      
    

- Cost: 100–500 gp based on floor tier (every 10 floors increases cost)  
      
    
- Availability: Limited to documented climbers of that spire.  
      
    

5. Echo Tokens – Simulate spire floors for training purposes. Used in:  
      
    

- Stillmarch Coliseum – Tests of endurance, divine/spiritual floors  
      
    
- Carthis Coliseum – Tactical, arcane, and puzzle simulations  
      
    
- Cost: 75–1500 gp per use per member (or free for Echoseeker members)  
      
    

7. Challenge Tokens – Temporarily modify a spire floor to a harder variant with greater rewards.  
      
    

- Cost: 40–500 gp based on challenge type  
      
    

### Challenge Tokens of the Climbers’ Society

Overview:

Challenge Tokens are single-use magical medallions that, when activated at the entrance of a spire floor, alter the climb. They are kept in sealed vaults and only distributed through guild auctions, climber trials, or earned through renown.

Each token belongs to a distinct category, and only one may be used per floor (unless a veteran climber invokes a Double Draw, available only to Echoseekers).

---

### Environmental & Condition Tokens

- Token of the Unbound Beasts  
      
    Increases the number, variety, and strength of monsters encountered on the floor. Adds elite templates or new mechanics.  
      
    
- Token of Withering Grace  
      
    Reduces all healing received by 50%. Affects magical, alchemical, and natural regeneration.  
      
    
- Token of Dragged Steps  
      
    Applies a magical slow to all climbers. Reduces movement speed, delay in reactions, and impedes swift actions.  
      
    
- Token of Weatherwrought Trials  
      
    Simulates extreme environmental conditions that change with each chamber. May include:  
      
    

- Dense mist (concealment, perception penalties)  
      
    
- Thunderstorms (lightning hazards, deafened condition)  
      
    
- Blizzards (freezing terrain, cold exposure)  
      
    
- Sweltering Heat (fatigue, fire vulnerability)  
      
    

- Token of the Eternal Trial  
      
    Prevents all rest, recovery, and respite between rooms. Climb becomes one continuous gauntlet.  
      
    
- Token of the Cursed Labyrinth  
      
    Each chamber imposes a different magical curse upon entry. These may range from misfortune effects, speech blocks, compulsions, or random mutations until the room is cleared.

## Climbers’ Society – Renown System

### What is Renown?

Renown is the core metric by which the Climbers’ Society measures a member’s achievements. It reflects both public prestige and internal status within the guild, and serves as the primary currency for gaining access to Challenge Tokens, elite climbing tools, and influence over guild operations.

---

### Earning Renown:

Climbers accumulate Renown Points (RP) through various high-risk and high-profile achievements:

  

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Action|Renown Gained|
|Reaching a new personal-best floor in any spire|+1 RP|
|Reaching a milestone floor (every 5th & 13th)|+2/+3 RP (stacked)|
|Completing a floor using a Challenge Token|+2–5 RP (based on token tier)|
|Completing a floor without healing outside of combat.|+2 RP|
|Completing Eternal Trial floors|+5 RP (flat bonus)|
|Being in a top-10 leaderboard party for any spire|+3 RP/month active|
|Winning a Coliseum Echo Trial (Stillmarch or Carthis)|+1–5 RP (depending on echo difficulty)|
|setting a new spire floor record|+5 RP (reviewed)|
|ascending the peak of a spire|+10,000 RP|

Note: Renown gains are recorded by the local Floor Marshals and confirmed by your attuned Spire Stone. All spire challenges are magically documented and reviewed.

  
  

### Renown Tiers:

  

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|Renown Title|Required RP|Privileges|
|Floorwalker|0|Base-level membership|
|Climber|50|Can bid on Ember-tier Challenge Tokens|
|Ascender|150|Access to Gale-tier Tokens, +1 free Echo Token/month|
|Trialshaper|300|Eligible for Obsidian-tier Tokens, invitations to elite climbs|
|Echoseeker|500|Access to vault relics, Mythral Tokens, and Double Draw privilege|

### Spending Renown:

Renown is not permanently spent—it represents lifetime accomplishments—but high-level access requires maintaining active status (i.e., climbing regularly or winning coliseum events).

#### Challenge Token Access via Renown:

Tokens are “purchased” at monthly lotteries, auctions, or direct commission using your renown tier as eligibility:

- Ember Tokens – Available to Climbers and above  
      
    
- Gale Tokens – Available to Ascenders and above  
      
    
- Obsidian Tokens – Trialshaper tier only; access subject to monthly rotation  
      
    
- Mythral Tokens – Echoseeker only; Vault draws limited to 1/month  
      
    

Each guildhall maintains a Challenge Vault where available tokens are posted on a leaderboard board and rotated based on demand and scarcity.

---

### Prestige & Titles:

Renown also determines social status and ceremonial honors in the Society:

- Special climbing gear or cloaks denoting your tier  
      
    
- Invitations to annual Climbers’ Gala in High Celest  
      
    
- Your name inscribed on public district leaderboards and the Hall of Echoes for high-tier climbers
