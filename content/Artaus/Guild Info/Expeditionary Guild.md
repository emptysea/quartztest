### The Expeditionary Guild

Motto: “Where Roads End, Our Journey Begins.”

#### Overview:

The Expeditionary Guild serves as the lifeline between Celest and the vast, untamed wildlands that lie beyond its walls. Unlike the Adventurers’ Association, which focuses on internal city contracts and spire operations, the Expeditionary Guild looks outward—its mission rooted in exploration, diplomacy, and reclamation. Whether uncovering long-lost ruins, navigating ancient forests, brokering trade with isolated settlements, or recovering relics from forgotten epochs, its members are the vanguard of Celest’s ambitions beyond its borders.

---

#### Guild Mandates:

- Exploration & Mapping:  
      
    Charting unknown regions, cataloging terrain, and establishing waystations and safe routes for future travel or expansion.  
      
    
- Settlement Relations:  
      
    Acting as ambassadors and mediators with non-Celestian settlements—some friendly, others wary, and a few openly hostile.  
      
    
- Archaeological and Arcane Excavation:  
      
    Unearthing long-buried ruins, sunken vaults, and dungeons lost to time. Teams often include scholars, mages, and scouts.  
      
    
- Trade & Resource Acquisition:  
      
    Establishing new trade agreements and securing exotic goods or natural resources not found within Celest’s borders.  
      
    
- Relic Recovery:  
      
    Seeking out artifacts, celestial remnants, or ancient technologies that may provide insight into Artaus’s lost history—or power for its future.  
      
    

---

#### Headquarters and Structure:

- High Celest – The Pinnacle Hall:  
      
    The central command center of the guild. Oversees long-range planning, relic documentation, and expedition approval. Built into the cliffs beneath the Spire of Creation, it includes a sky-lit council chamber and artifact vault.  
      
    
- Verdant Refuge – The Green Gate Lodge:  
      
    Serves as the gateway to northern jungles, forests, and Moutan passes. Specialists here include beastfolk scouts, druids, and nature-bound researchers.  
      
    
- Carthis – The Iron Archive:  
      
    Known for its deep records, artifact research, and logistical planning. Many expeditions are outfitted here with prototype tools and chartered caravans.  
      
    

---

#### Organizational Ranks:

- The Pathcarved Council:  
      
    A trio of Guild Masters—one from each headquarters—who vote on major undertakings and relay discoveries to Celest’s Council of Districts.  
      
    
- Wayseeker Captains:  
      
    Leaders of individual expeditions. Chosen for expertise in survival, diplomacy, or relic lore. Each wears a badge bearing the guild’s crest and their chosen expedition name.  
      
    
- Expeditionaries:  
      
    Guild operatives who specialize in a field—cartography, beast handling, relic extraction, survival, linguistics, etc.  
      
    

---

#### Guild Symbol:

A three-pronged compass ringed in thorned laurels, with a rising sunburst cresting behind it. Each prong represents one of the guild’s halls.

---

#### Cultural Reputation:

To some, the Expeditionary Guild is seen as courageous pioneers—heroes preserving the memory and ruins of a fading age. To others, especially isolationist groups, they are meddlers who risk disturbing ancient powers best left buried. Regardless, their findings have helped unlock portions of Artaus’s forgotten past

  
  

### Expeditionary Guild Membership & Dues

#### Initial Membership Fee:

- 25 Gold Pieces (one-time)  
      
    This fee covers:  
      
    

- Background check and vetting (often magical)  
      
    
- Issuance of the official Guild Seal Pendant  
      
    
- Entry in the Expeditionary Roster (kept at the Pinnacle Hall)  
      
    
- Access to training seminars and guild-sponsored briefings on known regions  
      
    

  

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|
|Initiate|Wayborn|5 gp|Access to public missions, shared transport, and general resource libraries.|
|Journeyman|Trailcarver|15 gp|Priority on guild expeditions, lodging at field camps, and gear discounts.|
|Veteran|Relicbound|50 gp|Access to restricted ruins, personal vault space, and hiring privileges.|
|Elite|Pathcarved|100 gp|Access to high-clearance ruins, relic registry rights, and vote in hall affairs.|

#### Optional Contributions & Costs:

- Expedition Sponsorship Fund:  
      
    Members may donate an extra 10 gp or more annually to help fund long-range, high-risk expeditions. Donors gain renown and may have their name inscribed on recovered maps or journals.  
      
    
- Special Licenses (Optional):  
      
    

- Beast Handling Permit – 8 gp/year (required for animal use in field camps)  
      
    
- Relic Handling Certification – 20 gp/year (needed to transport divine or arcane relics)  
      
    
- Outpost Charter License – 100 gp (one-time) to establish a semi-permanent base in a new region  
      
    

---

#### Failure to Pay Dues:

Members are given a 3-month grace period. After that:

- Their pendant is deactivated (removing access to guild resources)  
      
    
- Their records are moved to “Inactive” status  
      
    
- Reinstatement requires 10 gp + back dues + re-vetting
