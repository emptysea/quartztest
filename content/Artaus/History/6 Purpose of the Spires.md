Each Spire holds three layers of meaning:

1. Divine Seat – A holy monument where the influence of the spire’s patron celestials would anchor within the world.

2. Living Dungeon – Within each spire lies a labyrinth of ever-shifting trials—pocket realms crafted by the celestials themselves. These tests reflect their domains and values: monstrous battles, moral puzzles, arcane riddles, spiritual confrontations, and environmental extremes. No two entries are ever the same.

3. Path to Ascendancy – Climbing and surviving within a spire is seen as the highest form of achievement. Many complete their rite of passage to choose their class within the spires, discover divine callings, or forge legendary legacies. The most honored citizens of [[Celest|Celest]] are not those born into power—but those who proved themselves within the spires.
