Circa 1298 A.D

For thirty-seven years, the Prime Celestial has remained silent.

[[Argus|Argus]], the Architect of Creation and guide of the divine order, has missed not one, but two consecutive Divine Assemblies—a sacred gathering held every thirteen years where all celestials convene in harmony. Though it was not unheard of for [[Argus|Argus]] to abstain from a conclave now and then, his continued absence has begun to cast long shadows across the city of Celest and the world beyond.

With the next Assembly now only two years away and no sign of [[Argus|Argus]] returning, unease stirs across the districts. Fewer youth climb the Spires, and more delay their Rites of Passage, unsure of the gods’ gaze upon them. Whispers of imbalance echo behind temple doors and within noble halls, yet the remaining celestials present a steadfast unity to the public—one that strains beneath the weight of an unspoken fear.

The world endures. The districts still stand. But the question now lingers in every heart:  
What happens if [[Argus|Argus]] never returns?

Now at the dawning of the new year you prepare for your rite of passage, you are going to enter a spire and gain your attunement.
