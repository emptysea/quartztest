“When Celestials walked unmasked, and the world itself bent to their will.”

**~ Circa 0 to 500 A.D. (After Dawn)**

⸻

Overview

The Age of Great Wanderlust followed the sacrificial creation of Artaus. Though many celestials had given up their physical forms to forge the sun, moons, land, sea, and stars, their divine essence lingered, vast and unspent. Over time, each celestial learned to re-manifest—not fully as they once were, but in forms mortal eyes could behold.

For nearly five centuries, the gods wandered the lands they helped shape, their presence changing not only the earth but the hearts of those who beheld them. Entire civilizations rose in reverence to the celestials, while others fractured beneath the weight of divine contradiction. Mortals were taught art and war, love and secrecy, invention and balance. Great temples, shrines, and wonders were built during this time—but so too were deep grudges and ancient sins.

Yet, as centuries passed, discontent grew among the Celestials. The ancestries often fell to conflict, driven by pride, greed, fear, and difference. The world was not the harmonious wonder the celestials had hoped to see.
