Following the founding of Celest, the seven districts began to flourish. Cultures intertwined. Trade grew. Temples were raised. For the first time in Artaus’s history, peace lasted.

But peace, untempered, breeds stagnation.

In time, mortals lost their drive to grow. Heroes turned to leisure. Scholars stopped questioning. The ambitious became idle, and the divine—once deeply present in every facet of life—felt distant. The celestials, still watching from amongst their people, grew concerned.

And so, they created the Spires.

Each pair of celestials—one light, one shadow, one flame, one stone—pooled their essence once more. They raised seven monumental spires, each unique in shape, style, and spirit, one per district of Celest. To the mortals, they declared:

“Within these towers lie your trials. Within them, your truths. Climb, descend, delve—forge yourselves anew.”
