Within two centuries of their construction, the spires became woven into the soul of [[Celest|Celest]]. Climbing the spire of one’s home district is a rite of passage. Many undergo these trials as a declaration of adulthood, a step into their destined role in the world, or a pilgrimage of redemption.

The Spires serve as beacons of identity, challenge, and divine proximity—a chance for mortals to reach upward, not to become celestials, but to understand them.
