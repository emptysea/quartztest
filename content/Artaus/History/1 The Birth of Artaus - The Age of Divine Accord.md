In the beginning, the celestials were divided spread apart in the great abyss. Then [[Argus]]—the Prime Celestial, Patron of Creation, Nothingness, and Truth. From the quiet void of unformed potential Argus summoned thirteen celestials, each born from the infinite essence of the void. These celestials were more than divine; they were the living embodiments of the world’s many creations—of land and sea, of light and shadow, of knowledge and chaos, of beginnings and ends.

Together, under Argus’s guidance, the celestials forged the world of Artaus.

The Sacrifices that Shaped the World

In an act of unparalleled unity, the celestials gave of themselves to manifest the world’s foundation:

• [[Din|Din]] and [[Lun|Lun]] sacrificed their celestial bodies to become the twin moons, forever circling above—observers and anchors of arcane tides.

• [[The Night Mother|The Night Mother]], [[Leinunya|Leinunya]], and [[Nephtus|Nephtus]] cast their forms into the heavens, forming the night sky and stars—a dreamscape of luck, secrets, and beauty.

• [[Ramza|Ramza]] became the sun, a burning symbol of unwavering honor and steadfast light.

• [[Mykuril|Mykuril]], [[Teiggus|Teiggus]], [[Sunthara|Sunthara]], [[Droumond|Droumond]], and [[Mephestos|Mephestos]] shaped the physical world—its forests, mountains, oceans, deserts, plains, and the heart of fire beneath.

• [[Adonius|Adonius]], drawing upon the divine essence of all, breathed life into the mortal world, creating the known ancestries: Humans, Dwarves, Elves, Catfolk, Ratfolk, Tripkee, Gnomes, Halflings, Leshy, Minotaurs, Tanuki, Tengu, Vanara, and Beastkin.

• [[DaKrell|DaKrell]] used the essence of all others to fashion the Netherworld, where souls journey after death. There, [[DaKrell|DaKrell]] weighs each soul’s life against his Eternal Flame. The worthy ascend to their patron’s realm. The lost are given rebirth—to try again.
