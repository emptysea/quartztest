Central District - Borders all other Districts

#### “Where stone remembers, and stars still whisper.”

  

 Built atop and deep within the Spine of Creation High Celest is the beating heart of Celest—a district of soaring splendor and subterranean might. From the luminous heights of its floating citadel to the echoing depths of the dwarven halls below, this district is where divine intent, mortal governance, and ancestral labor converge.

Home to much of Celest’s high nobility, the Peaks balances grandeur with grit. Beneath the snow-capped mountains lie the Halls of Droumond, a vast dwarven underrealm carved by divine hands. Above, the Upper Celestial Peaks float and spiral in brilliant arcs, housing great archives, academies, and temples bathed in starlight.

Notable Locations

### Throne of Deepfire

Carved into the living rock above a magma vein, this obsidian throne radiates heat and ancient power. Here, the King Under the Mountain holds council and issues decrees, his rule anchored in stone and flame.

---

### The Crucible Eternal

The greatest forge in all of Celest, its flames are said to have been kindled by Droumond himself. Divine alloys, enchanted weapons, and sacred relics are crafted here by master smiths and flamebinders.

---

### The Hall of Remembrance

Ancestral statues and carved murals line this vast chamber, honoring fallen kings, spire climbers, and dwarven heroes. The names etched into stone whisper legacy and sacrifice to those who linger long enough to listen.

---

### The Veins of Droumond

These deep, echoing mines wind through glowing crystal caverns and veins of rare minerals. Guarded and blessed, they fuel Celest’s trade and echo with ancient chants of the mountain.

---

### Skyhold Citadel

Floating high above the peaks, this radiant citadel is home to the Eternal King. Its walkways shimmer with celestial energy, and its halls reflect starlight and prophecy.

---

  

### The Luminarium Archives

A sprawling library of divine tomes, moon-glyphs, and celestial edicts. The air within hums with magic, and only the most trusted scholars are permitted access to its sealed wings.

---

  

### Concordant Spire Academy

This elite training ground welcomes students from all districts to prepare for their Rite of Passage. Here, martial tradition meets divine purpose, blending philosophy, endurance, and celestial calling.

---

### The Grand Ecliptic

An open-air divine auditorium said to link the heavens and the mortal realm. Every 13 years, the gods gather here—though Argus has not returned for three cycles, a silence that grows heavier with time.
