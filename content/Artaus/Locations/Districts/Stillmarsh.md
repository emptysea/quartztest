Southwestern district - Borders [[Mystharra|Mystharra]] and [[Carthis|Carthis]]

“Where all paths end, and faith begins.”

Stillmarch is a solemn, radiant district devoted to honor, healing, faith, and the quiet certainty of death. Built at the border between life and what lies beyond, it is governed by reverence for two celestials: [[Ramza|Ramza]], Celestial of virtue, protection, healing, and family; and [[DaKrell|DaKrell]], the faceless Celestial of death and final judgment.

It is a place of pilgrimage and purpose—where clerics, paladins, and the devout from every corner of [[Celest|Celest]] come to pray, to learn, and to climb the district’s sacred monolith: [[Campaigns/Artaus/Locations/The Spire of Faith|the Spire of Faith]]. Said to test the body, spirit, and loyalty of the soul, the Spire is a crucible of character—and no climber returns unchanged.

Stillmarch holds little trade, but offers the greatest spiritual and restorative services in the city. Its healer houses are renowned, its orphanage beloved, and its courthouses feared and respected in equal measure.

### Notable locations

### The Shieldhall of [[Ramza|Ramza]]

Overseen by: The Voice of Protection  
A grand martial school and training yard where paladins, champions, and guardians of faith hone their bodies and minds. The grounds feature both outdoor sparring rings and sacred training halls etched with [[Ramza|Ramza]]’s tenets. Weaponry is blessed before use, and duels are settled with honor, never rage.

---

### The House of Enduring Grace

Overseen by: The Hand of Healing  
A revered healer’s sanctuary known across [[Celest|Celest]] for its miraculous recoveries. Its wards are fragrant with herbs and lit by soft divine auras. Alchemical brewers, divine medics, and clerics serve both the poor and the powerful. A lower wing manages medicine vendors and trains apprentice healers under careful oversight.

---

### The Halls Beneath the Veil

Overseen by: The Keeper of the Veil  
This solemn complex of tombs, mausoleums, and open-air memorial gardens winds beneath the district’s southern rise. Whispering winds move through towering statues of faceless guardians. Funerals here are silent but powerful, honoring both the body and the soul as it crosses into [[DaKrell|DaKrell]]’s domain.

---

### The Pilgrim’s Reach

Overseen by: The Warden of the Way  
The first stop for those intending to ascend [[Campaigns/Artaus/Locations/The Spire of Faith|the Spire of Faith]]. This cloistered plaza includes dormitories, meditation paths, shrines to all celestials, and quiet gardens where pilgrims prepare their minds. The Warden keeps detailed scrolls of every climber's entry, prayer, and return—or lack thereof.

---

### The Temple of Living Flame

Overseen by: The Flamebearer  
A circular temple where ritual fires are kept alight in honor of [[Ramza|Ramza]], [[DaKrell|DaKrell]], and the eternal soul. From here, flamebearers travel across Stillmarch to light ceremonial braziers. The temple’s heart houses the Cradle Flame, said to reveal untruths when someone speaks falsely nearby.

---

### The Archive of Sacred Words

Overseen by: The Binding Voice  
A vast, vaulted library filled with scrolls, divine law, historical decrees, and interpretations of sacred doctrine. The Binding Voice also presides over oaths, contracts, and spiritual binding rites within its adjoining courthouse wing. The library is open to all—but only few are allowed to speak aloud within.

---

### The Radiant Chorus Amphitheater

Overseen by: The Chorus of Light  
An open-air, acoustically perfect amphitheater surrounded by flowering trees and carved stone. Here, ceremonies of devotion, seasonal celebrations, and reenactments of celestial myths are performed. During moments of mourning or celebration, the Chorus leads the people in prayer-songs heard throughout the district.

---

### The Lanternhome

Overseen by: The Lantern of Hope  
A humble but radiant orphanage built around a courtyard garden, where children orphaned by Spire climbs, illness, or war are raised under sacred protection. Every child raised in Lanternhome leaves with a handwoven pendant blessed by all nine Concord members. Older children often serve as aides to the pilgrim houses and healer halls.

---

### The Judgment Spire

Overseen by: The Bell of Reckoning  
A towering courthouse joined to the Archive, where divine judgment is delivered on mortal matters. It is both tribunal and temple, where prayers for justice are spoken aloud and the condemned are given one last chance to speak before [[DaKrell|DaKrell]]’s altar. A great bronze bell tolls only for verdicts rendered in full consensus.

---

### [[Campaigns/Artaus/Locations/The Spire of Faith|The Spire of Faith]]

Rising at the heart of Stillmarch, [[Campaigns/Artaus/Locations/The Spire of Faith|the Spire of Faith]] is a sacred tower created by the celestials [[Ramza|Ramza]] and [[DaKrell|DaKrell]]. It challenges climbers with trials of honor, courage, belief, and spiritual conviction. No two paths within are the same, and each floor reflects the soul of the one who climbs it.

It is said that to ascend the Spire is to walk between light and shadow—and only those who carry both in balance will reach its heights.
