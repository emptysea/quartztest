District: Stillmarch | Conquered: 1236 A.D.

Caelus Veyne is the only mortal to ascend a Spire alone. A humble soul raised among the dead, he walked the path of both Ramza and DaKrell. At the summit, both Celestials offered him a boon—he refused. In doing so, he was chosen to become the first High Hierophant by divine will. His faith remains the cornerstone of Stillmarch’s spiritual life, and his climb is the most recent—and most revered—conquest in Celest’s history.
