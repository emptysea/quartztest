District: [[The Verdant Refuge|The Verdant Refuge]] | Conquered: ~900–934 A.D.

Three figures ascended: a leshy druid born from beneath the Greenheart, a wildshape bearkin, and a half-elf ranger whose path remains cloaked in mystery. They completed the Spire’s trials in full and received sacred boons—immortality, a seed of the Spire itself, and something unspoken. They left [[Celest|Celest]] after their triumph, searching for a place to plant the future. They are honored every spring as the Greenwardens.
