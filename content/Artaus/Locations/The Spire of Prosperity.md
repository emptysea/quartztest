District: [[Vallendale|Vallendale]] | Conquered: ~735 A.D.

Four adventurers entered its obsidian halls. Only two returned—a halfling rogue and a human warrior—both forever changed by the journey and silent about what transpired within. The names of the other two were never shared, out of respect or remembrance. Some say they chose to remain behind, seeking truths or destinies beyond mortal reach. What is certain is this—the Spire was conquered, and the echoes of that ascent still shape [[Vallendale|Vallendale]]’s legacy.
