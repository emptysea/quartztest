## 🗓 Act1: session 1

📍 Location: The Spire of ???

🎯 Objective: Complete the Rite of Passage

---

### 📖 Summary

What was meant to be a solitary journey through their chosen Spires became something far stranger. The party—six strangers—awoke together inside the mysterious Spire of Creation, united by confusion and necessity. With little choice but to cooperate, they pressed on toward the heart of the unknown, each clinging to hope that the Rite of Passage would still grant them an attunement. From acidic corridors to divine confrontations and arcane puzzles, they survived trial after trial until, at last, they reached the Attunement Chamber—and chose their destinies.

---

### 🧭 Key Events

- The party awakens together inside the Spire of Creation, breaking the tradition of solitude.  
      
    
- They survive a hazardous hall of acidic slime using a nullifying bucket and battle several oozes to retrieve a keycard.  
      
    
- Cross a bubbling mud pit via a narrow path; [[Ander Vaelthorn|Ander]] leads and activates a lever that stabilizes the room.  
      
    
- Descend a strange stairwell to find three prisoners: Victra, her sickly brother Laus, and a mysterious swordsman named Solaris.  
      
    
- The party chooses to save Victra and Laus. Solaris escapes on his own using a void-forged blade.  
      
    
- [[Adonius|Adonius]] appears in a throne room, confronting Solaris. Solaris stays behind to fight the celestial while the party escapes.  
      
    
- The group solves a challenging lever puzzle using ingenuity and a 20-foot ladder. Victra vanishes through the door alone.  
      
    
- In the Attunement Chamber, each character binds themselves to their chosen path:  
      
    

- [[Nessimae Ellywick|Nessimae]] – Oracle  
      
    
- [[Grimfang Nightbloom|Grimfang]] – Thaumaturge  
      
    
- [[Seraphine Nightbloom|Seraphine]] – Alchemist  
      
    
- [[Ander Vaelthorn|Ander]] – Barbarian  
      
    
- [[Fallo Sunfang|Fallo]] – Barbarian  
      
    
- [[Benny "Beans" Barns|Beans]] – Rogue  
      
    

- The group navigates a trapped maze and defeats enchanted statues. [[Ander Vaelthorn|Ander]] finds a hidden stash with gold and a dagger.  
      
    
- In a fort-like arena, they battle a goblin war band led by a brutal orc. [[Ander Vaelthorn|Ander]] is nearly slain three times.  
      
    
- After a hard-won victory, the party exits through a swirling golden portal… and emerges in Vallendale.
